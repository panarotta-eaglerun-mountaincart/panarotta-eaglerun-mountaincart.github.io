PANAROTTA EAGLE RUN

This mountain recreational sporting activity carries a risk of falls, collisions and injury. The natural trail is mainly dirt and gravel and may contain holes, stones, erosion, water channels, dips, changing slopes and other mountain obstacles. Ride cautiously and responsibly, at a speed suited to your ability, the trail, visibility and other people. Taking delivery of a cart implies that you have read and accepted the rules.

1. Eligibility

You may drive your own Mountain Cart only if you are at least 13 years old, at least 150 cm tall, weigh no more than 120 kg, are physically and mentally fit to operate it, and can use the brakes correctly and control the cart independently. Age and height are cumulative requirements: both must be met.

Children aged 5 to 12 inclusive must ride on the same Mountain Cart as a responsible adult.

Riders aged 13 to 16 inclusive may drive their own Mountain Cart, provided a responsible adult follows them along the trail on another Mountain Cart.

Do not give a Mountain Cart to anyone who does not meet the requirements.

2. No passengers

Each Mountain Cart is for one person, except for children aged 5 to 12 inclusive, who must ride on the same Mountain Cart as a responsible adult, as described in rule 1.

3. Minors

A parent, guardian or other authorised adult must hire the cart and take responsibility for a minor. The responsible adult declares that they have checked the minor’s physical, technical and psychomotor ability to operate the cart.

Staff may refuse a minor access if they consider the rider unable to control the cart safely.

4. Helmets are mandatory

Wear a helmet correctly throughout the entire descent. Fasten it before setting off and keep it fastened until the end. Starting or continuing the descent without a helmet is prohibited.

5. Clothing and footwear

Sturdy, closed shoes that fit securely are mandatory. Slippers, flip-flops, open sandals, high heels and footwear that can slip off easily while riding are prohibited.

Do not carry pointed, sharp or other objects that could be dangerous in a fall. Clothing appropriate for sporting activity and the weather is recommended.

6. Mandatory safety briefing

Before the first descent, every rider must attend the safety briefing given by staff. It covers at least: using the cart; brake operation; correct riding position; behaviour on the trail; speed management; safe following distance; stopping; trail signs; the walking section; and procedures for accidents or breakdowns.

You may not use a Mountain Cart unless you have received and understood the safety instructions.

7. Checking the Mountain Cart

Before setting off, with staff support, check that both brakes work correctly, that the cart is in good general condition and that there are no visible faults.

If there is any doubt, fault or malfunction, do not use the cart and notify staff immediately. By taking delivery of the cart, you declare that you have checked that it works.

8. Using the brakes

The cart’s braking systems must be used correctly. When braking, use both brakes at the same time and apply them progressively.

Abrupt manoeuvres, deliberate skids, intentional wheel locking or other behaviour that could cause loss of control are prohibited.

Reduce speed before bends, hairpins, steep sections, narrow sections, junctions, obstacles and areas with poor visibility.

9. Speed

Keep your speed low enough to remain in full control, stop within the distance visible ahead, negotiate bends and changes in slope safely, and react to the sudden presence of people, animals, vehicles or obstacles.

Slow down further on wet ground, gravel, potholes, stones, erosion or water channels, in poor visibility or adverse weather, and when other riders are present. Your speed must always suit your personal ability.

10. Safe following distance

Keep at least 20 metres behind the cart ahead. Increase this distance on steeper sections, wet or uneven ground, in poor visibility, when the rider ahead is moving slowly, and near bends and hairpins.

Do not set off until there is sufficient distance from the preceding cart.

11. No overtaking

Overtaking is prohibited along the entire route unless staff directly instruct otherwise in a specifically designated safe area.

Keep your place and adapt your speed to the cart ahead.

12. Do not stop on the trail

Do not stop along the route unless genuinely necessary. If you are forced to stop: move to the edge if possible; choose a spot clearly visible to approaching riders; keep the track as clear as possible; and report any problems immediately.

Stopping is strictly prohibited immediately after a bend, within a bend, after a crest, on hairpins, in narrow or steep sections, or anywhere with insufficient visibility.

13. Walking section

Panarotta Eagle Run has a section of approximately 50 metres where you must push the cart by hand. In this marked section, staying seated or travelling on the cart uphill or downhill is strictly prohibited.

Bring the cart to a complete stop; get off; proceed only on foot; push it while keeping it under control at all times; and get back on only after passing the sign marking the end of the walking section.

Do not let others tow or push you, or use the cart in any way other than described.

14. Mandatory route

Use the cart only on Panarotta Eagle Run and on sections expressly authorised for it.

Do not leave the trail, take shortcuts, enter unauthorised paths or roads, enter closed areas, alter the route, or ignore barriers, signs or other instructions.

15. People, animals and vehicles

The trail is in a mountain environment. Always be prepared for pedestrians, hikers, staff, work vehicles, agricultural or forestry vehicles, domestic or farm animals, wildlife and occasional obstacles.

When people, animals or vehicles are present, you must slow down immediately and stop if necessary. Do not chase, frighten or disturb animals along the route.

16. No riding under the influence

Riding under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, psychotropic substances or medicines that may impair attention, perception, reaction time, balance or motor skills is strictly prohibited.

Staff may refuse or stop use even if they only suspect physical or mental impairment.

17. Health conditions

You may not ride if you are pregnant; have cardiovascular conditions incompatible with the activity; have spinal or back problems incompatible with the activity; have illnesses, injuries or physical conditions that could be aggravated by vibration, impacts or the activity; or have physical or mental conditions that compromise safe driving.

Each rider is responsible for assessing their fitness to participate. If in doubt, you must not ride.

18. Prohibited behaviour

The following are prohibited: riding without holding the cart correctly; deliberately excessive speed; races or competitions; overtaking; deliberate skids; zigzagging; intentional collisions with other carts; towing or pushing other carts; being towed; using phones, cameras or other devices while riding; headphones or earbuds that reduce awareness of the surroundings; abandoning the cart on the trail; tampering with the cart; handing it to another person during the descent; or any behaviour that could endanger yourself or others.

19. Accidents, falls and breakdowns

In an accident, fall, collision, damage or breakdown, you must stop the descent when necessary; make yourself and, as far as possible, the cart safe; avoid obstructing approaching riders; notify staff immediately; and follow their instructions.

Every incident involving a collision with people, objects or other carts must be reported, even if it appears to have had no consequences.

20. Signs and staff instructions

Follow all signs, barriers, instructions along the route and directions given by staff. Staff instructions given for safety reasons take priority over normal operating arrangements.

21. Operator’s right to refuse use

The operator may refuse to hand over a cart or stop its use if they consider conditions unsafe.

Access may be refused or withdrawn for failure to meet age, height or weight requirements; unsuitable physical or mental condition; dangerous behaviour; failure to follow the briefing, rules or staff instructions; or misuse of the cart.

A cart may be withdrawn immediately for a breach of safety rules.

22. Weather and trail closure

The operator may delay, suspend or stop the service, or close all or part of the trail at any time when safety could be affected. Reasons include heavy rain, thunderstorms, lightning, strong wind, hail, poor visibility, deterioration of the surface, obstacles, works or maintenance, technical faults, instructions from authorities or any other circumstance considered incompatible with safety.

23. Damage to the cart

Use the cart, helmet and all supplied equipment with care. Report any damage or fault immediately.

Within the limits of applicable law, the rider is liable for damage caused by misuse, negligence, failure to follow these rules or staff instructions, or intentional conduct.

24. Rider’s responsibility

You are responsible for your behaviour throughout the activity and must take all necessary precautions to prevent harm to yourself, others, property and equipment.

You acknowledge that the activity takes place on natural mountain terrain that can change or become uneven due to its natural characteristics, wear and weather.

Use is subject to these rules and your responsibility for your own conduct, without affecting the operator’s responsibilities that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

25. Knowledge of the trail and risks

Before use, the rider declares that they have been informed that the trail has a natural surface in an alpine environment. Gravel, loose stones, rocks, holes, dips, erosion, water channels, changes in surface or slope, mud, vegetation and other natural obstacles may appear, including unexpectedly.

These conditions require cautious riding and continuous adjustment of speed.

26. Acceptance of the rules

Taking delivery of and using the cart requires reading and understanding the rules, attending the safety briefing, accepting the conditions of use and committing to follow staff instructions.

For minors, the responsible adult must also read and accept the rules. Breaking even one provision may result in immediate termination of the activity and withdrawal of the cart.

Images must respect the dignity and decorum of those depicted and must not be used for other purposes.
