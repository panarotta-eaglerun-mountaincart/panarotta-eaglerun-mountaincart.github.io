What data we process

For the adult completing the form: first name, last name, date and place of birth, email address. Why we process it and on what legal basis

a) Recording acceptance of the rules and managing the Mountain Cart rental, including age requirements: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

b) Sending you the confirmation email for your declaration with the link to confirm your email address: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

c) Keeping the declaration to prove acceptance and protect the controller’s rights in case of accidents, disputes or requests from authorities: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

d) Recording and using photo/video images for the purposes of section 27 of the rules: only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Mandatory or optional

The data under a), b) and c) is needed to complete online acceptance; if you prefer not to provide it, contact the staff. Photo/video authorisation is optional: refusing it does not prevent participation. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to lagorai2002@gmail.com; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier uses.

How and where it is stored

Data sent with the form is recorded in a Google spreadsheet of the controller, and the confirmation email is sent through Google’s email service. Google acts as service provider on behalf of the controller; data may be transferred to the United States under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. The form pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub), which logs technical browsing data such as the IP address for security and operation. Only authorised staff can access the data; it is not disclosed or used for profiling or automated decisions.

How long

Declarations are kept for as long as needed to manage the rental and afterwards for the period in which rights or claims connected with the activity can be asserted, according to statutory limitation periods. Photo/video authorisation remains valid until withdrawn.

Minors

Minors’ data is provided by the adult completing the form, who declares that they are entitled to do so. Photo/video authorisation for a minor may only be given by a parent or a person with parental responsibility.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability at any time, and withdraw consent, by writing to the controller. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).
